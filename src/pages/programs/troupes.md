          # Troupes

          Our five acting troupes allow our students to refine and perfect the skills they learned in their acting and dance classes. We offer two middle school troupes – Gaithersburg and Junior – as well as three high school troupes – Theatricals, Shakespeare, and Senior. With the exception of Theatricals, all troupes require students to audition in May. Anyone who is interested in a troupe must first take a year of acting.
